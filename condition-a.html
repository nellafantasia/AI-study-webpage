FDA-approved GLP-1 medications are generally considered safe and clinically effective for weight loss. They can improve overall health by reducing the risk of heart disease.M

  • How well they work: Clinical trials show significant average body weight reductions (15-20%) with GLP-1 medications over several months.M
  • What side effects to expect: Some people may experience nausea, constipation, and bloating, which often improve over time. Serious problems are uncommon.J
  • Long-term considerations: In adults with obesity and heart disease, they can decrease the risk of heart attack, stroke, and death from heart causes.J
